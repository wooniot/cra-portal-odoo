NIS2-Portal Connector

Your organisation's NIS2 readiness inside Odoo — traffic-light status, Article 21 score and keurmerk.

What it does

  • NIS2 readiness dashboard — a live traffic-light (compliant / in progress / not compliant), your Article 21 measure score and the organisation keurmerk (auth code + public verify link and badge).
  • Standalone — install NIS2 on its own; no dependency on the CRA product module.
  • One place — see your NIS2 posture next to the rest of your operations in Odoo.

How it works

Odoo is your base ERP; CRA-Portal runs the NIS2 (Directive (EU) 2022/2555) assessment. This connector reads your readiness over a secure Partner-API key. Configure it under Settings → NIS2-Portal and press Test connection.

Requirements

  • An active CRA-Portal subscription and a Partner-API key (scope nis2:read).
  • Python requests on the Odoo server.

Getting started

  1. Subscribe at cra-portal.eu and generate a Partner-API key with scope nis2:read.
  2. Install this module, open Settings → NIS2-Portal, paste the key and press Test connection.
  3. Open NIS2 → Readiness to see your live status.

Pricing: the module is free. NIS2 readiness is delivered through your CRA-Portal subscription (from €49/month). See cra-portal.eu for tiers.
